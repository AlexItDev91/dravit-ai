# Human use by activity and family

Use this map to decide what to inspect in the saved model and what to ask about in the brief. It makes no claim that the server can compute every question. Resolve the exact family/preset schema first; derived data and existing checks remain the only program evidence.

| Family/context | Purpose and relationships to preserve | Use and evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Project, Site, Axis, Level and types | Identity, datum, ownership, bindings and catalog definitions | These organize geometry; they are not routes or human-use spaces |
| Room | Logical use zone at its saved Level | Confirm purpose in the brief, actual physical boundaries and entry; room polygon/name/finish is not an accessibility check |
| Wall, Foundation, Column, Slab | Enclosure/support, actual joins, hosts, cutouts and Level bindings | Inspect XYZ and dependent openings; visual alignment is not a structural design or load check |
| Roof, Dormer, Fronton, Roof Window | Existing envelope assembly and actual hosted parts | Verify parent/bindings, envelope interactions and relevant views; no invented nested action, roof access or performance claim |
| Door, Window, openings | Passage, privacy, light/view or stated special purpose | Read host/side/configuration; distinguish static infill, motion, approach and aperture; functional checks may be absent |
| Stair, Railing | Supported vertical travel and edge assembly | Need a continuous path, approaches, exit and slab hole; rail/stair objects alone do not certify safety or access |
| Bed | Sleeping, getting in/out and requested attendant access | Required free sides follow the brief; a permitted wall side is valid; Bunk geometry does not prove ladder/headroom usability |
| Wardrobe, floating shelf, storage | Retrieval, opening and storage activity | Rear may meet a wall; front reach, doors/drawers and service need evidence; drawing usage zones are not a validator |
| Chair/bench, armchair, sofa, table | Sitting, rising, interaction and shared use | Assess functional fronts, pull-out space where relevant, approach and simultaneous seating; no universal perimeter gap |
| Kitchen module, refrigerator, sink | Preparation, storage, washing and equipment use | Preserve exact catalog composition and functional fronts; address task sequence and opening conflicts; no assumed service capacity |
| Shower cabin, bathtub, toilet, washing machine, water heater | Hygiene, laundry, supply and maintenance | Consider approach, transfer/use, doors/lids and service as distinct activities; connectors are not plumbing performance proof |
| Television, office desk/chair, piano/drum kit | Viewing, focused work or playing | Respect viewing/working orientation and interaction, approach, cables/service if required; noise, lighting and equipment design require separate facts |
| Gym strength/cardio/accessory, wall mirror | Exercise and equipment use | Preset drawing zones/mirror position do not establish saved reach, motion or hosted-wall semantics; use only actual facts |
| Terrain, Pavement, Pool | Outdoor surface/use and existing boundaries/levels | Confirm actual Site/Level/offset and intended route/use; render does not establish slip, drainage, water safety or terrain accessibility |
| Fence, fence access, Canopy | Boundary, controlled passage or shelter | Inspect actual Fence/span host and moving access if known; Canopy is its existing decorative assembly, not certified structural shelter |
| Exterior furniture, Umbrella | Sitting, rest, waste handling, shade or outdoor work | Assess approach, front/top use and service; fixed lounge/canopy/umbrella geometry does not imply moving mechanisms |
| Vegetation, Transport | Planting and neutral parked vehicle form factors | Exact preset/asset and placement only; no invented growth, road manoeuvres, species, sweep or sunlight analysis |
| Generic Shape/Group | User-defined geometric composition | Shape/group names do not turn them into semantic doors, stairs or furniture; group transforms/membership follow existing schema |
| Pipe network/segments/fittings/nodes/ports | Existing routed assembly and actual provider bindings | Read pinned catalog and saved XYZ connectivity; topology does not prove flow, pressure, power, reach or maintenance access |
| Underlay/IFC/reference context | Reference evidence with separate provenance | Visibility/absence in preview differs from model ownership; copied reference data does not authorize import/write bypasses |

For every relevant activity consider these distinct needs without forcing irrelevant requirements onto the project:

- User's stated abilities and desired independence; approach, posture, reach and any assistance.
- Entries, movement, turns, transitions, deliveries and access to exterior areas within the known model boundary.
- Opening, closing, withdrawing and returning parts or objects through the actual required sequence.
- Shared activities, privacy, social contact and a place for retreat; shared service routes should respect the brief's private boundaries.
- Storage location, frequency of retrieval, cleaning, maintenance and eventual equipment replacement.
- Work/rest separation, sensory clarity, orientation, control and the user's cultural or household preferences.
- Light, view, noise, air, thermal comfort, moisture and odour as separate questions requiring adequate data and calculations.
- Future adaptation and conflicts between space, storage, daylight, privacy and cost; explain choices before discarding a requirement.

Do not infer an age, medical condition or household preference from a drawing. Do not turn a qualitative concern into a numeric pass/fail threshold. A technical shaft missing a documented service hatch is a service uncertainty; it is not an excuse for a bedroom missing its required entrance. A permitted windowless pantry or open passage remains valid within that brief.

This is the E8 human-use synthesis, not a normative catalog. For provenance/applicability use the source table in [spatial reasoning](spatial-reasoning.md). [CEUD Elements and Systems](https://universaldesign.ie/uploads/publications/Section-4-Elements-and-Systems_2024-09-24-122859_xnua.pdf), 2015 edition §4.1, informs adaptation/service questions; it does not supply unverified structural capacities. [WHO's 2018 announcement](https://www.who.int/news/item/26-11-2018-housing-impacts-health-new-who-guidelines-on-housing-and-health) establishes housing-health risk domains only; its full guidelines were not extracted into this package.

When a question lacks facts or a checking owner, state exactly what remains unknown/unsupported. Preserve it through repair and the final report instead of treating silence as success.

For Bathroom activities, consult the Wall-support recipe in [catalog recipes](catalog-recipes.md). Verify the represented installation, its actual rear face and mounting height. A basin or built-in shower placed inside the correct Room can still lack Wall support. Keep freestanding variants and washing machines distinct; placement findings are recommendations, not Save admission rules.
